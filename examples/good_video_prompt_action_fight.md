# GOOD — VIDEO PROMPT (ACTION / SPARRING, 15s, text-to-video) — EVIDENCE_ONLY
> Tầng Design. Dùng để hiểu cơ chế, không copy chủ thể/bối cảnh/motif sang prompt khác (Example Quarantine Law).

## Craft + Exchange Plan (tóm tắt)
| Trường | Giá trị |
|---|---|
| playbook | G02, trường phái `WIDE_LONG_TAKE` |
| hook_type | Motion surprise: cú đá mở màn ở 0–2s |
| exchanges | 2 exchange + 1 counter trong 15s (trong ngân sách 52.3) |
| axis | Fighter A (áo đỏ) luôn frame-left, Fighter B (áo xám) luôn frame-right |
| camera | một đường: track ngang chậm, 28mm, một cận 85mm cho phản ứng |
| signature | cú counter duy nhất ở 9–10s có slow motion ghi giây |
| sound | giày trên bê tông, hơi thở, một impact chính, lặng 0.5s trước counter |

## Prompt (runtime, English)
```text
16:9, 15s, 720p. Real-time speed except 9–10s. Staged, non-graphic sparring choreography.

Fighter A: woman, 30s, red hoodie, black joggers, stays frame-left. Fighter B: man, 30s, gray tank top, stays frame-right. Empty concrete parking garage at night, painted pillar behind them, puddle between them. They start three meters apart in guard stance.

0–3s: Wide shot, 28mm, slow lateral track right to left. A steps in and throws a front kick toward B's midsection; B steps back and the kick misses by a hand's width. Ends with: two meters apart, both in guard.
3–7s: B closes to arm's length and throws a right jab toward A's left shoulder; A blocks with her left forearm, her rear foot slides back through the puddle and water splashes. Ends with: A braced, B's arm retracting.
7–9s: Both circle one step, same sides of frame. A half-second pause, only breathing.
9–10s: Slow motion. A ducks under B's hook and pushes his chest with both palms; his tank top ripples and he stumbles back two steps.
10–13s: Real-time. Close-up, 85mm, on B catching his balance against the pillar, then nodding once.
13–15s: Wide shot. Both separate and settle into ready poses, three meters apart. Ends with: both still, breathing hard.

Lighting: hard cold fluorescent overheads, one warm sodium lamp behind A, wet floor reflections, fine film grain.
Audio: sneakers scuffing concrete, fabric snaps, breath, <one muted palm impact at 9.5s>, distant traffic hum. No music. No dialogue.
Constraints: exactly two people. A always frame-left, B always frame-right. No weapons, no blood, no injury, no extra cuts, no costume changes, no merged limbs, five fingers per hand.
```

## Vì sao đạt (Design)
- Mỗi exchange có initiator, khoảng cách, đường đi, điểm chạm và bằng chứng lực (nước bắn, áo gợn, loạng choạng).
- Mỗi segment kết thúc bằng khoảng cách + tư thế, nên nối clip không bị reset.
- Một đường camera, khóa frame side hai người, slow motion đúng một đoạn có mốc giây.
- Ngôn ngữ dàn dựng, không bạo lực đồ họa.
