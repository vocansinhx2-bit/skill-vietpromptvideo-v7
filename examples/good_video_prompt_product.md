## Prompt (runtime, English)
```text
@Image1 is the insulated bottle: keep its shape, matte navy finish and label exactly. @Image2 is the woman: keep her face and hair only.

A young woman on a Colorado hiking trail at golden hour, 9:16 vertical.

0–3s: Medium shot, 50mm, handheld. She pulls a dripping plastic bottle out of her backpack, the backpack fabric visibly soaked. Ends with: she frowns at the wet backpack.
3–7s: Close-up, 50mm. She drops the plastic bottle into a trash bin and takes the @Image1 bottle from a side pocket with her right hand. Ends with: the bottle held at chest height, label facing camera.
7–11s: Macro, 100mm, static. Her left hand unscrews the cap, then turns the open bottle fully upside down. No water falls. Ends with: the bottle held upside down, dry trail dust below it.
11–15s: Wide shot, 35mm, slow dolly back. She screws the cap on, slides the bottle into the dry backpack and walks up the trail away from camera. Ends with: her figure small against the ridge.

Lighting: low warm sun from camera-right, long shadows, rim light on the bottle edge, natural contrast, fine grain.
Audio: wind through pines, footsteps on gravel, water dripping <sfx> at start, cap click <sfx> at 11s. Light acoustic guitar (music) starting at 7s.
Constraints: one woman only, same outfit, bottle label always readable and unchanged, right-handed grip, real-time speed, no text on screen.
```

## Vì sao đạt (Design)
- Reference có vai rõ: lấy gì / không lấy gì.
- Cài (ba lô ướt) và trả (bình lật ngược không rò) là bằng chứng quan sát được, không cần lời quảng cáo.
- Macro cho signature moment; nhạc vào đúng lúc chuyển từ vấn đề sang giải pháp.
